Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Drive RAID is software that each person installs on their own server. It is not a hosted service, and the author operates no server that receives user data.

When you connect a Google account, the application receives a token that lets it use the Google Drive API on your behalf. It uses that access only to create, read, update and delete files inside a folder named DriveRAID in your own Drive, and to read the free space in the account. The token is stored on your own server; your files stay in your own Drive.

The application does not send your data, file names or token to the author or to any third party. It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can delete your files at any time, and revoke the application's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
